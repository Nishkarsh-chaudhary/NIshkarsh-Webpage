# What I Do

## Technical Project Coordination

I coordinate projects across their complete delivery lifecycle. This includes establishing scope, identifying stakeholders, defining milestones, planning work, monitoring dependencies, managing risks, and ensuring that teams remain aligned with the intended outcome.

My responsibility is not limited to monitoring tasks. I work to create clarity around what is being built, why it matters, how it should behave, and what conditions must be met before it can be considered complete.

## Product and Requirements Management

I translate business requirements, meeting discussions, design concepts, and stakeholder expectations into structured product documentation.

My requirements work can include:

- Functional Requirement Documents
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Product workflows and status models
- Roles and permission definitions
- Business and validation rules
- Error, empty, and edge-case states
- System dependencies
- Integration requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Release-readiness and definition-of-done criteria

This documentation gives design, engineering, and QA teams a shared source of truth.

## Agile Delivery

I support Agile delivery through sprint planning, backlog organization, daily coordination, progress tracking, issue resolution, testing alignment, and retrospectives.

I help teams maintain momentum without losing visibility into quality, risk, or business priorities.

## Cross-Functional Collaboration

I act as a bridge between:

- Clients and internal delivery teams
- Product owners and engineering teams
- UI/UX designers and developers
- Frontend, backend, QA, DevOps, and leadership
- Business requirements and technical constraints

My objective is to reduce ambiguity, prevent avoidable rework, and make decisions visible to everyone involved.

## Quality Assurance and UAT Coordination

I help define acceptance criteria, prepare testing scenarios, coordinate defect resolution, manage feedback cycles, and support user acceptance testing.

Before release, I focus on confirming that the product is not only technically functional but also aligned with the original business requirement.

## Stakeholder Communication

I provide stakeholders with structured updates covering progress, decisions, risks, blockers, dependencies, and next steps.

I am comfortable communicating with clients, managers, directors, technical leads, and executive stakeholders. My communication style is transparent, concise, and focused on actions and outcomes.

For example, when a visitor repeatedly views a product or shows sustained interest in a category, the platform can evaluate that behavior, identify an eligible campaign, apply priority and frequency rules, and show a relevant popup.

This creates a connected journey:

Visitor activity → behavior evaluation → campaign selection → popup presentation → submission → profile update → tag assignment → audience update → analytics

### Project Overview

AI Office Analytics is a proof-of-concept for understanding how workplaces are used through existing CCTV or IP-camera infrastructure.

The concept uses anonymous person detection and object tracking to measure space activity without facial recognition or identity-level monitoring.

### My Contribution

- Defined the product purpose, scope, constraints, and success criteria.
- Documented meeting-room occupancy requirements.
- Defined desk-utilization and dwell-time measurement workflows.
- Specified visitor entry and exit analytics.
- Designed requirements for space-utilization heatmaps.
- Defined local data-storage and dashboard requirements.
- Structured the system into camera, detection, tracking, analytics, storage, and reporting layers.
- Created a phased implementation and testing plan.
- Defined measurable targets for latency, performance, accuracy, privacy, reliability, and usability.
- Identified risks involving lighting, camera stability, object-tracking accuracy, processing performance, and scope growth.

### Proposed Technology

- Python
- OpenCV
- YOLO
- ByteTrack or DeepSORT
- SQLite
- Streamlit
- NumPy

### Privacy-First Approach

The concept intentionally excludes facial recognition. It works with anonymous detections and temporary tracking identifiers, allowing space-level insights without attempting to identify individual employees or visitors.

### Potential Applications

I convert business expectations into user flows, functional requirements, user stories, business rules, acceptance criteria, and technical discussion points.

I coordinate QA and UAT, compare implementation against acceptance criteria, manage feedback, and support defect prioritization.

# Working Principles

## Clarity Before Execution

Clear requirements reduce rework. I ensure that scope, behavior, dependencies, and success criteria are understood before development advances too far.

## Business and Technology Must Stay Connected

A feature is valuable only when it solves a real user or business problem. I continuously connect technical decisions with operational outcomes.

## Risks Should Be Visible Early

I identify and communicate blockers, assumptions, unresolved decisions, and dependencies before they become release problems.

## Quality Is a Shared Responsibility

Quality is not limited to the QA stage. It begins with requirements and continues through design, implementation, testing, release, and monitoring.

## Documentation Should Support Decisions

Useful documentation should clarify the product and accelerate delivery. It should help teams make decisions, test behavior, and understand why something was built.

## Progress Must Be Transparent

Stakeholders should understand what has been completed, what is in progress, what is blocked, and which decisions are required.
